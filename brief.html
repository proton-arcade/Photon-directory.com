Rev. 3 · still arguable

DIT.EDITDesign brief

DIT.EDITThe site should feel like a shop notice board that someone keeps tidy, not a product page. Dark room, lamp on, blue paper, red pin. Everything you can click is ringed in yellow so there is never a guessing game.

DIT.EDITTone

DIT.EDITPlain-spoken, first person plural, willing to admit failure. The homepage says outright that one project was abandoned, because a shop that has never lost a job is a shop that is lying. Sentences vary in length on purpose. Some are long and wander through the reasoning. Some don't.

DIT.EDITNothing on the page is filler. Every name, price and date is a real decision that can be swapped for yours by clicking the text.

DIT.EDITColour

RoleValueWhere
Page ground#0a0d16Near-black with blue in it, not grey
Panel#0e1422Quote box, tables, drawing slots

Deep red#7d1220Button hover, the pin, the left edge of notes

Text#e8e3d9Warm off-white on near-black — high contrast, not glare

DIT.EDITNo gradients on buttons, no glass, no shadows. Flat fills and hard 2px borders.

DIT.EDITType

DIT.EDITTwo families, both installed on every machine since forever: Georgia for headings, Courier New for body and interface. Serif over monospace is the oldest pairing on the web and it still reads. Hierarchy is strict: 38px page title, 24px yellow section head, 17px item head, 16px body, 13px asides. Nothing in between, ever.

DIT.EDITBody copy is capped at about 62 characters per line and sits left, indented away from the page edge rather than pinned to it.

DIT.EDITSpace and alignment

DIT.EDITThe column is 860px and centred; the text inside it is not. Sections use three different gaps — 26px, 54px and 96px — chosen by how much of a breath the section needs, so the rhythm is uneven on purpose. Indents step in at 26px and 58px. Corners are 2px. Everything else is square.

DIT.EDITThe only motion on the site is the scrolling notice strip under the nav, because a notice board really does change. No scroll reveals, no hover choreography.

DIT.EDITDrawings to make

IMAGE 1 — home, under the opening (800 × 420)

Ink line-work, no shading. The long desk from above: scuffed wood, two mismatched mugs, stack of stapled zines with the top one open, a ruler, a stub pencil, a beige CRT pushed half out of the right edge. Coffee ring lower left. Deliberately off-centre.

IMAGE 2 — home, beside the process table (360 × 520)

Pencil wireframe pinned crooked to corkboard with one red pin. Handwritten labels: "words", "picture here?", "price list". One box crossed out. A thumbprint smudge in the corner.

IMAGE 3 — logo mark, optional (square, 200 × 200)

A long radio wave drawn as one continuous hand-inked line crossing a rectangle of paper, with the line thickening where the pen pressed. Red dot where it exits the frame.

DIT.EDITScan at 600dpi, drop the files in as PNGs, replace each dashed slot with an img tag and paste the alt text written under the slot on the homepage.

DIT.EDITHow editing works

DIT.EDITAny text carrying the yellow DIT.EDIT tag can be clicked and typed over. Nothing structural is editable, so you cannot break the layout by writing too much or too little. Delete the tag itself once you're happy with the words.
