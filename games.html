A cabinet, but a piece of furniture rather than an arcade machine: four legs, a shelf underneath with a shoe box of spare cables on it. The CRT sits inside the frame, screen on, showing a snake of filled squares and one filled square apart from it. Two buttons and a joystick ball on the sloped panel. One corner of the top has a cup ring. Draw the shadow only under the cabinet, nowhere else.

Two things I will say up front

The typing drill uses a list of 700 common words rather than a dictionary, because a drill that teaches you to type quixotic is teaching you nothing about the words you actually write.

Pong's machine reacts to the ball with a fixed, slightly stupid delay. It is not tuned to be hard; it is tuned so that a person who has not played since 1985 can still win a set. If you find it too soft, the number is 0.075 in js/games.js.

Nothing in the cabinet phones home, so there is no leaderboard. Your best run is the only record that exists.
