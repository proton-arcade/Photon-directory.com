Tools · one textarea and a drawer

Notes, kept where you type them.

This is not a notes application. It is a pad: a stack of plain text, the top one open, saving as you go. No markdown preview, no tags, no syncing, nothing to sign into. The word count is here because that is the only measurement a note has.

How it saves

MomentWhat happens
You typeThe counter updates on the next key. Nothing else, because repainting the drawer per keystroke is how pads get laggy.
You pause400 ms after the last keystroke the whole drawer is written to localStorage and the corner says saved with a time.
You leaveA final flush runs on beforeunload, for the case where you typed and walked off inside the same 400 ms.
You come backThe most recently touched note is open. Pinned ones sit above everything.
It fills up200 notes or 5 MB, whichever comes first, then the oldest unpinned note is dropped and the bar tells you. Cap is in NOTEPAD.MAX_NOTES.

Back up anything you would hate to lose. That is not modesty, it is what localStorage is: a per-browser cache a “clear site data” can take away, and a browser under disk pressure may take away first.
