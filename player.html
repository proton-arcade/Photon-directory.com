Player · no server, no upload, no telemetry

Load a folder. It plays what a browser can play, and explains the rest.

Containers are paperwork: MKV, MP4 and MOV describe where the tracks sit. Codecs are the picture itself: H.264, VP9, DivX, ProRes. Browsers are generous with the paperwork and strict about the picture, so film.mkv may play perfectly and rip.mkv may do nothing. This page tells you which one you have before you go looking for another player.

Practical part

What plays, what does not, and what to do about it

FileContainerVerdict on a normal browser
.mkvMatroska Plays when the picture is H.264, HEVC or VP9/VP8 and the sound is AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC or PCM. Fails on AC-3 and E-AC-3 in Firefox, on anything DivX or Xvid, on DTS and on TrueHD. Chapters and multiple audio tracks are ignored — the browser only ever takes the default pair.
.mp4 .m4vISO The safest thing you can hand a browser. Still fails if the video is MPEG-4 Part 2 or ProRes, and a file whose moov box is at the end cannot be seeked until it is fully read.
.webmMatroska, WebM codecs VP8/VP9/AV1 with Opus or Vorbis. If you shot something for the web, this and mp4 are the only two fights worth having.
.movQuickTime Usually fine if the codec inside is H.264 or HEVC. Screen recordings from a phone are the common one that is HEVC and therefore browser-dependent.
.aviRIFF/AVI, 1992 Never. Not a codec problem — no browser has an AVI demuxer. ffmpeg -i in.avi -c copy out.mkv is a copy and takes seconds.
.flv .vob .mpg .tsold / MPEG Demux into something first. The streams inside are often fine; the paperwork is not.
.mp3 .m4a .ogg .opus .flac .wavaudio All fine. The stage collapses to a black panel and the deck keeps working, which is what you want for a 90 minute recording.

The frame grab button writes the current frame out as a PNG at full decoded resolution. It is the fastest way to check whether a file is worth keeping: a badly encoded source shows its blocks at 1:1 immediately.

Three things this player will not do

  • It will not decode a codec the browser has no decoder for. No ffmpeg is loaded, because a 30 MB WASM transcoder in a folder that is trying to be light is a bad trade. If you want that, say so and I will add it behind a checkbox.
  • It will not pick audio tracks. Matroska lets a file carry six; <video> lets you have the default one.
  • It will not play DRM anything, and it will not open a stream from the internet. This page has no fetch in it at all.

Where your position is kept

The player writes the name, the byte size and a coarse timestamp of every file you start into localStorage under photon.resume.v1, capped at the 60 most recent. That is how it can offer to put you back at 41:12 tomorrow. It is names and numbers only, it never leaves the machine, and deletes it right now.
