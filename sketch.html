How the export works

The sheet's pixel size is the slot's size, so export writes what you drew with no scaling at all. at 2× doubles it for a print or a crisp @2x. Changing the hole mid-drawing resamples what you have already drawn to the new sheet — nearest neighbour, so pencil stays pencil and does not turn into fog.

A PNG with alpha off, paper colour filled first: a drawing on #e8e3d9 will sit in the dashed box exactly as it reads here. If you want the paper to be transparent, flip to night paper and the export keeps that.

Seven lines that help

  • Draw the outline first with width 1 or 2; the brief wants line-work, not shading.
  • Keep the focal object off-centre. Every slot in the brief says do not centre it.
  • One red element per drawing, and only one — that is the pin, the corner, the label.
  • Use the grid for the paper edge and nothing else; a drawing on a grid looks like a diagram.
  • Straight edge (or hold shift while dragging) for tables, frames, the CRT box.
  • Trace underlay: drop a photo of the real desk in, dim it, draw over it, then turn it off.
  • Export, then look at it at the size it will be shown. Pencil that reads at 800 px turns to mush at 360.

What is kept, and for how long

The pad stores strokes as points in localStorage, not a flattened image. That is why undo survives a reload and why the file stays small — a full drawing of a desk is around 90 KB of numbers. It is still a cache and not a backup: when you are happy with a sheet, export the PNG and put it in img/. Then the drawing is a file in the repo, which is the only place anything on this hub is safe.
